# Scenes

Every scene from the standard works worth stepping into. A scene earns a place when it has a real location, a single moment, and something to walk around and see. Sermons without a vivid setting, genealogies, and doctrine-only chapters are left out.

Order is chronological within each book.

## What the engine has to handle

- **Crowds**: King Benjamin, Christ at Bountiful, Sermon on the Mount, Pentecost.
- **Water and ships**: Nephi's ship, Jaredite barges, Red Sea, Galilee storm, Waters of Mormon.
- **Night and fire**: Laban, Ammonihah, Nephi and Lehi in prison, Carmel, the fiery furnace.
- **Interiors**: Liberty Jail, Carthage Jail, the Last Supper, the Kirtland Temple.
- **Visions**: tree of life, Moses 1, Jacob's ladder, D&C 76. Settings that are symbolic, not physical.
- **Divine figures**: faceless or very simple, as in the prototype.
